## What it does

Destinos Únicos sells gifts for a specific occasion: a birthday, an anniversary,
a date that had to be marked somehow. The site has a public face and a private
one.

The public side opens with the shop's pitch, a short section about who is behind
it, a handful of featured gifts read straight from the database and a slider of
customer reviews. The full catalogue lives on its own page, where gifts are
filtered by occasion — romantic, special date or birthday — and each one opens
in a detail card.

There is no checkout, on purpose: every gift and the catalogue itself lead to
WhatsApp with the message already written, which is where this shop actually
takes its orders.

## How it is built

Next.js with the App Router and React, in TypeScript, styled with Tailwind CSS.
The catalogue, the reviews and the profiles live in Supabase, and each screen
reads them from the server through a small query module, so the browser
downloads no data-fetching code of its own. The pictures of the gifts are stored
in Cloudinary. The whole thing is deployed on Vercel.

The route groups split the three worlds the site has — public, authentication
and administration — without that showing up in the URLs.

## The private area

The shop keeps its own catalogue. Behind a login backed by Supabase Auth, there
is a form to create a gift, edit it, replace its picture or take it off the
catalogue without deleting it, so a seasonal gift can come back next year.

Access is checked before the page is even rendered: anyone without a session who
asks for the admin area lands on the login page, and anyone with one who asks
for the login page lands on the admin. The picture that is uploaded travels to
Cloudinary and comes back as a URL stored next to the gift.
