## What it is

A single-page site for a notary office: services, who they are, frequently asked
questions, appointment request and contact. Everything a visitor needs before
picking up the phone, in the order they need it.

## The opposite of the other one

This and La Cava Negra were the same technical test, and that is the point of
having both here. One sells atmosphere in the dark; this one sells trust in
daylight — navy and gold, a photograph of a real signing, and a booking button
repeated wherever the visitor might decide.

Measured on Lighthouse in September 2026: 90 performance, 88 accessibility, 96
best practices and 91 SEO.
