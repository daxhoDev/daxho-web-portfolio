## What it is

A single-page site for a restaurant that sells atmosphere before it sells food:
black background, gold accent, and photographs that carry all the weight. Story,
menu, gallery and booking, in that order, with a single call to action repeated
in the header and the hero.

## Why it looks like this

It was a technical test with a deadline, and the brief asked for character. The
darkness is the decision: on a black page, a plate photographed against a dark
background stops being a picture on a website and starts being the website. The
type stays quiet — one weight, generous spacing — so nothing competes.

Measured on Lighthouse in September 2026: 88 performance, 92 accessibility, 96
best practices and 91 SEO.
