## What it does

A supplier of HVAC equipment, parts and appliances selling to trade and
residential customers. The visitor searches by model number — which is how this
trade actually looks for a part — filters the catalogue by brand and
availability, and sees on every card whether the item is in stock before
clicking.

Each product page shows the price per unit, live stock and a quantity stepper.
Buying does not require an account, and neither does coming back later: with the
order number and the e-mail used at checkout, anyone can track an order.

The whole storefront has a light and a dark theme.

## My part in it

Built from scratch, end to end, and still in active development. The catalogue
is being loaded, so some products are still waiting for their photographs.
